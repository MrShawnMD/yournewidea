Start to finish

From raw stone to your table.

Four steps, done by hand, one board at a time. This is what you're giving.

I.

Cut from real slate

Every board starts as a raw slab of natural stone. Each one is cut to size on a wet saw, so the edges and the grain are never quite the same twice.

II.

Laser-etched with our mark

The Biscoff Box mark is etched into the face of the stone itself — not printed, not stuck on. It's part of the board for good.

III.

Oiled by hand

Each board is rubbed with food-safe oil by hand. The slate deepens to a rich black and the etching comes alive.

IV.

Into your home

Packed in-house with Lotus Biscoff cookies, cookie butter and two wooden spreaders, then onto the table — and back out for every gathering after.
